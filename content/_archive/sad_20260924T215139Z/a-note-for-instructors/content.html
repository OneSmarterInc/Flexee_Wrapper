A note for instructors

This book is designed to be taught from, and it is deliberately incomplete on its own.

Adopters receive slide decks for a full term, answers to every review question, teaching notes, and guidance on fitting the material to terms shorter than fourteen weeks.

The book also has a companion simulation. Student teams are engaged as analysts by a fictional food bank, interview nine AI-played stakeholders against a fixed budget of analyst hours, and submit models that are scored on requirements coverage, model quality, traceability, stakeholder satisfaction, and the cost of rework. The rework multiplier rises as the term goes on, which makes the economic argument of Chapter 1 something students feel rather than read.

Nothing about the simulation appears in these chapters, and the book stands without it. But the two were designed together, and the combination teaches something neither does alone.
