# Acknowledgements

A book this short takes longer than it looks, and it took more people than the title page suggests.

The team at Flexee built everything around it. The slide decks, the studio materials, the simulation the book is designed to sit beside, and the reader you may be holding this in. Very little of that is visible when the work is done well, which is the nature of it. My thanks to all of them.

My students, over more years than I will specify, taught me that the eight-hundred-page textbook was not being read. It took me a while to hear it. They were right, and this book is what happened once I stopped defending the alternative. My teaching philosophy is an applied one, and that is what I wanted this book to be. Learn, absorb, apply — in this case the principles and concepts of systems analysis and design.

Most students deviate away from analysis and fast-forward to development. It is a task to slow them down and teach that good analysis and thought prior to development is equally important, if not more. Hopefully I can push that lesson to all my students.

To **MMB**, who carried the parts of the year that had nothing to do with this book, so that I could spend it on this book. To **HVN** and **AJ**, who asked how it was going often enough that I had to keep having an answer.

**Errors that remain are mine.** If you find one, I would rather hear about it than not. The book is versioned, and corrections reach adopters.
