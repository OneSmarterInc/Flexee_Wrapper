# Flexee for Faculty — Quick Start

Read the book, run objective-linked exams, and keep grades — all from inside your D2L
(Brightspace) course. No separate Flexee account.

## Getting started (about five minutes)

1. **Open the Flexee link in your D2L course.** Your Brightspace admin enables it once; when
   you add it, you pick your book (e.g. *Introduction to MIS*) from a short list.
2. **Click it.** You're signed in automatically through D2L — no extra password — and you
   land on your **section dashboard**.
3. **Your roster loads from D2L.** Students who are in your Brightspace course appear
   automatically; there's a *Sync roster from LMS* button to refresh anytime. (You can also
   share a join code or upload a CSV if you ever need to.)

## What you can do

- **Reading.** Students read the book inside D2L — chapters, figures, a side table of
  contents, and a bookmark that resumes where they left off.
- **Exams.** Build one from the question bank: a random draw (e.g. "5 from Chapter 4, mixed
  difficulty") or a fixed set. Set attempts, timing, and whether answers/explanations show
  immediately or after the exam closes. Different students get different draws.
- **Mastery of learning.** See how the class and each student are doing **by learning
  objective**, not just by exam — and map objectives to your own syllabus outcomes so results
  roll up the way your program reports them.
- **Gradebook.** Weighted totals across exams and any manual columns you add. One click,
  **Push grades to LMS**, sends them to the D2L gradebook; or export a CSV.

## What your students see

They click the same D2L link, get signed in automatically, and land in the book. They take
exams there and see their scores (and explanations, per your setting). Their grades show up
in the normal D2L gradebook once you push them.

## Good to know

- **One D2L course = one Flexee section.** Roles come from D2L — you're the instructor,
  they're students, automatically.
- **The optional AI reading tutor is off by default.** It can be enabled with your own API
  key; it only ever sees the book, never exam answer keys.
- **Everything is per course.** Your section's roster, exams, versions, and grades are yours;
  another instructor's section is independent, even for the same book.
